Directions

Worked out from the planned route. Tap a line to see it on the chart. Bearings are compass points, distances in nautical miles (1 nm = 1.85 km). Not for navigation: follow the beacons and the boat's plotter.

Chart layers

Satellite: Esri World Imagery, toned down. Depths, land, beacons, names, walls, bridges and bridge clearances: Australian Hydrographic Office ENC Online, not for navigation, fetched when the job link was made; Coomera River beacons from the MSQ Beacon to Beacon maps. Depth now = the shallowest charted depth where you are on the route plus the tide. Tide: MSQ predictions for Southport (Gold Coast Seaway), heights above lowest astronomical tide, the chart datum; north of Runaway Bay the Paradise Point rule (1 hour later, 0.87 of the height, MSQ Southport port procedures). Bridges: clearance above highest astronomical tide (1.97 m at the Seaway, MSQ tidal planes 2025) corrected for the tide now. Tide flow arrows: direction only, estimated by rule (flood from about 1.5 h after low water to 1.5 h after high water at the Seaway, Griffith University Broadwater model study). Wind: Open-Meteo forecast model at your position. Zones: approximate, drawn from the MSQ schedule of speed limits, Gold Coast waterways. The boat's own chart plotter stays the primary.
